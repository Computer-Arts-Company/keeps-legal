# Support & feedback

<p class="lede">Need help with Keeps, found something that feels off, or have an idea for making it better? Send us a note.</p>

<a class="contact-button" href="mailto:nitesh@computerarts.company?subject=Keeps%20Support%20%26%20Feedback">Email Keeps support</a>

## Helpful details

If you’re reporting a problem, including the following will help us investigate it more quickly:

- what you were trying to do;
- what happened instead;
- your iPhone model and iOS version; and
- a screenshot or screen recording, when appropriate.

Please avoid including passwords or other sensitive information.

You can also contact us directly at [nitesh@computerarts.company](mailto:nitesh@computerarts.company).
